04 / The next step

Tell us which trade-off is acceptable.

If Go is the hard requirement, we should favor profiles 01 and 03. If the harder part is keeping non-cloud systems healthy, profile 02 deserves a close look. Your answer will make the next batch much sharper.

Three questions for the call
  1. Is Go the primary production language, or one part of a broader engineering stack?
  2. Does “non-cloud” mean bare-metal or on-premise infrastructure, or development through a VPN into Linux-hosted systems?
  3. What seniority, working-hours overlap, and monthly budget should the search target?
